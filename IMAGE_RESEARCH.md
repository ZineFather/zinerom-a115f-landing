# ZineROM device-image research

This record identifies the source behind each product image currently used in the roadmap. Product images are for device identification only; ZineROM does not claim ownership or a reuse license. An official source page verifies product identity, not permission to republish the image.

## Device records

### Galaxy A11 — SM-A115F

The selected PhoneMore render shows the front, side, and rear of the handset, not a box. The source page identifies the SM-A115F/DS family, cross-checked with [Samsung Levant support](https://www.samsung.com/levant/support/model/SM-A115FZKDMID/). Image: [PhoneMore product render](https://cdn.phonemore.com/content/2020/jpg/14177.jpg). Source: [PhoneMore Galaxy A11 specifications](https://www.phonemore.com/specs/samsung/galaxy-a11/sm-a115fds-32gb2gb/).

### Galaxy A52s 5G — SM-A528B

An earlier image attributed to an eBay listing was visually wrong: it showed a different Galaxy A-series design. It was replaced. The selected front/back image comes from [Amazon UK's exact SM-A528B product listing](https://www.amazon.co.uk/Samsung-Galaxy-SM-A528B-Double-Android/dp/B09CHDTLJN), which identifies the Galaxy A52s 5G and displays the correct centered selfie camera and four-camera rear housing. Samsung's own [A52s 5G product page](https://www.samsung.com/ie/business/smartphones/galaxy-a/galaxy-a52s-5g-awesome-mint-128gb-sm-a528blgceua/) confirms model SM-A528B and the physical design. A Samsung Mobile Press front/rear gallery is also documented at [the official media-assets page](https://www.samsungmobilepress.com/media-assets/galaxy_a52s_5g); the selected card image is the matching marketplace product render, not an official Samsung file.

### Galaxy A25 5G — SM-A256E

### Galaxy A34 5G — SM-A346E

### Galaxy A15 — SM-A155F

The website currently uses a clean Galaxy A15 handset image sourced from a Boost Mobile catalog result. The device itself is shown, not its packaging; however, the source image's exact regional SKU was not independently confirmed. Samsung's official [SM-A155F Galaxy A15 page](https://www.samsung.com/uk/business/smartphones/galaxy-a/galaxy-a15-blue-black-128gb-sm-a155fzkdeub/) was checked for the target model. A separate Samsung image candidate for the exact 4G model is [the official camera-feature render](https://images.samsung.com/is/image/samsung/p6pim/uk/feature/164965940/uk-feature-smartphones-539629006?$FB_TYPE_K_JPG$); it includes promotional camera labels, so it was not selected for the clean device card.

### Galaxy A16 LTE — SM-A165F

Samsung's [official Galaxy A16 page](https://www.samsung.com/levant/smartphones/galaxy-a/galaxy-a16-black-128gb-sm-a165fzkgmea/) identifies product code SM-A165FZKGMEA and describes front/back product views. The selected image is the model-specific Samsung product-gallery render: [direct image asset](https://images.samsung.com/is/image/samsung/p6pim/levant/sm-a165fzkgmea/gallery/levant-galaxy-a16-sm-a165-sm-a165fzkgmea-thumb-544188100?$0_0_PNG$). Samsung publishes this gallery render at 330×330; the site crops transparent margins and resizes it for display. This is the LTE model, not the A16 5G image initially considered.

### Galaxy A17 — target variant to confirm
