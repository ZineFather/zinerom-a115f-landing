For consistent display, source images were resized and re-encoded to WebP. Whitespace was trimmed where needed; separate front/rear images for the A11, A52s, and A53 were placed side-by-side on a clean background. No device geometry or product features were added or altered. Where a source render is shared at family level, the catalog still identifies the exact network variant in text.
